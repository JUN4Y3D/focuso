# FOCUSO — Premium Single-Product Website Prototype

Act as a world-class senior product designer, ecommerce UX designer, visual designer, and conversion-focused brand designer.

Design a complete, polished, high-fidelity responsive website prototype for **FOCUSO**, a modern Muslim productivity and personal-growth brand from Bangladesh.

The website must strictly follow the supplied FOCUSO brand guidelines.

This is NOT a generic ecommerce store.

FOCUSO currently has ONE product:

**FOCUSO — Premium Undated 60-Day Muslim Productivity Planner**

Until additional products are introduced, the entire website should remain intentionally focused on this single product.

The experience should feel like a premium product launch page combined with an extremely smooth direct-purchase experience.

---

# 1. PRIMARY WEBSITE GOAL

The user journey should be extremely simple:

**Land on FOCUSO → See the planner → Understand the problem → Understand how the planner works → Explore the actual product → Build confidence → Order smoothly**

The website has one primary business objective:

**Sell the FOCUSO planner without making the experience feel aggressive, sales-heavy, cluttered, or like a generic online store.**

Every design decision should support this journey.

Do not build unnecessary ecommerce complexity.

---

# 2. BRAND FOUNDATION

FOCUSO is:

**A modern Muslim productivity and personal-growth brand designed around real Muslim life.**

Its purpose is to help Muslims bring more:

- structure
- consistency
- intention

into everyday life through thoughtful, practical tools.

The brand begins with a premium, undated 60-day planner designed for Bangladesh.

FOCUSO brings things people normally manage separately into one system:

- goals
- daily planning
- study/work
- responsibilities
- habits
- Salah
- Qur'an
- personal growth

The central brand idea is:

**Plan what matters.  
Stay consistent.**

The current product promise is:

**Plan what matters. Stay consistent. Make the next 60 days count.**

# 15. LAYOUT SYSTEM

Use disciplined grids.

Desktop:
- max content width roughly 1200–1320px
- strong alignment
- large whitespace
- balanced asymmetry where appropriate

Mobile:
- approximately 20–24px page margins
- single-column clarity
- comfortable vertical rhythm

Use an **8px base spacing system**.

Major section spacing can range approximately 80–140px desktop and 64–96px mobile depending on composition.

The website should breathe.

Do not fill every empty area.

Whitespace is part of the FOCUSO identity.
